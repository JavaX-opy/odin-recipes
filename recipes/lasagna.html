Lasagna

Description

A classic layered pasta dish with meat sauce, creamy ricotta, and melted mozzarella.

It takes a little time to assemble, but it's perfect for feeding a group.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 500g ground beef
  • 700ml tomato pasta sauce
  • 250g ricotta cheese
  • 300g shredded mozzarella
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Boil the noodles until just tender, then drain.
  3. Brown the beef with the onion and garlic, then stir in the sauce.
  4. Layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta, and mozzarella in a baking dish. Repeat.
  5. Top with mozzarella and bake for 30 to 40 minutes until bubbly.
